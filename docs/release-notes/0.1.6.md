# WMAPP 0.1.6

Private beta preparation for iOS and the universal browser FIPS transport.

- Includes the native iOS FIPS Packet Tunnel, with start, stop, repair and
  diagnostics controls in Setup.
- Gives approved browser applications a generic FIPS transport with explicit
  origin consent, exact endpoint and peer pinning, streaming and cancellation.
- Remembers scoped transport and signing policy decisions. Each approved
  authentication request is freshly signed for its exact URL, method and body.
- Retains the Tower browser compatibility adapter over the generic transport
  while existing consumers migrate.

## What to test on iOS

- Create/import and unlock the signer, then check that locking revokes browser
  access and that another origin needs its own approval.
- Enable FIPS and respond to Apple's VPN consent prompt. Load a known `.fips`
  WApp and ordinary HTTPS, then exercise stop, restart and repair.
- Check background/foreground recovery, screen lock and Wi-Fi/cellular changes.
- Export and clear diagnostics from Setup after a failed connection.

Physical iOS transport and VPN acceptance requires the checklist in
[Native iPhone FIPS](../deploy/ios-fips.md). These notes describe the beta's
scope; they do not establish that a build has been published to TestFlight.
